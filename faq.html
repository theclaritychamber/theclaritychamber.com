Frequently asked questions about tarot readings

About Readings

What can I ask about?

You can bring questions about relationships, career, life decisions, personal growth or any other area where you'd like perspective and guidance.

Does Loveleen use other forms of divination?

No. Loveleen works exclusively with tarot cards.

Will the reading tell me exactly what will happen?

Tarot is used as a tool for reflection, perspective and insight. A reading does not guarantee future outcomes or replace your own judgement and decisions.

Live Video Readings

How does a live reading work?

Your private session takes place in real time through Zoom or Google Meet. A booking number is emailed as confirmation, then you pay. The booking is not held unless payment is received. Times are in Australian Eastern Time (Sydney). A written summary (PDF) is included.

What happens if I need to reschedule?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the appointment for a full refund, or to reschedule free of charge. A cancellation or reschedule with less than 24 hours' notice is not normally eligible for a refund.

What happens if I cancel less than 24 hours before my appointment?

A cancellation less than 24 hours before the appointment is not normally eligible for a refund. Reasonable discretion may be applied in genuine exceptional circumstances.

Recorded Video Readings

How does a recorded reading work?

Choose your package, submit your questions and Loveleen will prepare a personalised recorded tarot reading addressing your questions.

When will I receive my recorded reading?

The recording is delivered by email within 3 business days of your questions being submitted on the booking form.

Is the written summary included?

Yes. A written summary (PDF) is included with every live and recorded reading, at no extra cost.

What is the bonus insight?

The recorded reading includes an additional insight beyond the submitted questions, where appropriate.

Booking & Payment

How do I book a reading?

Choose your reading type, select your package and complete the online booking process. For live readings, you'll also select an available appointment time. For recorded readings, you'll submit your questions on the booking form. A booking number is emailed as confirmation, then you pay. The booking is not held unless payment is received.

What payment methods are available?

Payment is by card through Stripe. A booking number is emailed as confirmation, then you are taken to payment. The booking is not held unless payment is received.

Will my payment details be secure?

Payments are processed by Stripe. The Clarity Chamber does not receive or store your full card details.

Before Your Reading

How should I prepare?

Think about the questions or situation you'd like to explore. You don't need to prepare anything complicated. Bring an open mind and be as clear as you can about what you'd like guidance on.

Can I ask more than one question?

Yes. For recorded readings, choose the package that provides the number of questions you'd like answered. For live readings, you can discuss multiple questions within the time available.

Important Information

Is there an age requirement?

Readings are available to people aged 18 years and over.

Are tarot readings a substitute for professional advice?

No. Tarot readings are intended for personal reflection, perspective and guidance. They are not a substitute for medical, legal, financial, psychological or other professional advice.

What is the cancellation policy?

A live reading cancelled at least 24 hours before the appointment is fully refunded, and rescheduling is free. A recorded reading cancelled within 24 hours of submitting your questions is fully refunded. After that, and within 3 business days of submitting them, the refund is 50%. Some circumstances may be considered with reasonable discretion.
