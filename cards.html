The Cards

A full deck has seventy-eight cards. Twenty-two Major Arcana map the larger themes of a life. Fifty-six Minor Arcana describe the weather of ordinary days — work, feeling, thought and the material world.

They are offered here as a quiet reference, not as prediction. Choose a group, then a card, for a grounded meaning in the spirit of The Clarity Chamber: direct, thoughtful, and free of fear.

Major Arcana

Beginnings, choices, endings, and the moment the picture comes clear.

Wands

Initiative, heat and the will to begin. Where energy wants to move.

Cups

Feeling, relationship and inner weather. What the heart is doing.

Swords

Thought, truth and difficult clarity. What must be said or decided.

Pentacles

Body, money, craft and time. What can be built and kept.
