Tarot card meanings — a quiet reference

A full deck has seventy-eight cards. Twenty-two Major Arcana map the larger themes of a life. Fifty-six Minor Arcana describe the weather of ordinary days — work, feeling, thought and the material world.

They are offered here as a quiet reference, not as prediction. Open a group, then a card, for a grounded meaning in the spirit of The Clarity Chamber: direct, thoughtful, and free of fear.

Tap a group to see every card. Open one for a short meaning and one thing to practise. Notes are the upright sense Loveleen works from; she reads the card as it falls.
