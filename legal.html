Privacy Policy

The Clarity Chamber (“we”, “us”), operated by Loveleen Sarao, respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles where they apply.

What we collect

When you book, pay, email, call, use a feedback form, or use the website, we may collect: name, email, phone, booking number, reading type and package, preferred date, time and platform, questions and remarks you submit, payment status (not your full card number), and messages you send us. The website may also collect basic technical data such as pages visited, through Google Analytics.

How we use it

We use this information to provide and manage readings, send confirmations and calendar invites, process or refund payments, deliver recordings, respond to you, improve the service, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information for marketing.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to run the service:

Stripe — payment processing. Card details are handled by Stripe, not stored by us. Stripe’s terms and privacy policy apply to that payment.

Google (Workspace, Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Forms, Apps Script, Analytics, Google Meet) — bookings, email, calendar invites, files, forms and site analytics. Google’s terms and privacy policy apply to those services.

Zoom — if you choose Zoom for a live reading. Zoom’s terms and privacy policy apply to that session.

GitHub — hosting of the public website.

Squarespace — domain registration and DNS.

We do not control how those providers handle data once it is on their systems.

Recordings and readings

Live sessions are not routinely recorded by us. Recorded readings and written summaries are prepared for you and sent to the email you give. They are for your personal use. Please do not share or republish them. We keep copies only as needed to deliver the service and handle follow-up.

Storage and security

Information is stored in Google Workspace and related tools. We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. No online system is completely secure.

Access and questions

You may ask to access or correct your information, or raise a privacy concern, by emailing loveleensarao@theclaritychamber.com.

Cancellation, Rescheduling and Refund Policy

This policy applies to bookings made through theclaritychamber.com. It does not remove rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law.

Live Video Readings

Give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule.

24 hours or more before the appointment
A 100% refund will be provided if you cancel. You may reschedule once without a fee, subject to available times.

Less than 24 hours before, or a no-show
A refund is not normally available. The session may be treated as used.
