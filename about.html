Loveleen Sarao, tarot reader in Australia

Welcome to The Clarity Chamber — a short introduction to the cards.

In November 2021 Loveleen completed the Tarot Master’s Program at the Academy of Tarot, India — a private training school founded by Mehakleen Sachdeva (Mehakleen Kaur), now based in India.

Loveleen is an Advanced Tarot Practitioner who approaches every reading with honesty, intuition, warmth and care.

Her approach is direct and honest while remaining thoughtful and empathetic. She uses tarot to help clients explore their questions, gain perspective and better understand the patterns influencing their decisions.

Loveleen is comfortable reading on every topic and believes a meaningful reading should leave you with greater clarity—not fear or confusion.

The Approach

  • Direct & Honest Clear, straightforward guidance without unnecessary mystery or sensationalism.
  • Intuitive Reading the cards with experience, intuition and attention to the individual situation.
  • Grounded Keeping the focus on your real questions, circumstances and perspective.
  • Empowering The purpose of a reading is to help you understand, reflect and make your own informed choices.

The seal

The seal is the room.

A gold circle holds the session in. Four amethysts mark the directions; clear stones sit between them. Amethyst for a quieter mind, clear stone for seeing. Inside, two columns and an arch open onto night: a crescent, low cloud, and a fixed star above the door.

Clarity here is not a floodlight. It is enough light to name what has been moving underneath, and a point to steer by once it is named.

The Clarity Chamber is that doorway. Loveleen Sarao holds the room.
