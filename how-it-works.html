How It Works

  • Choose your format Select a live video reading or a recorded video reading, and the area you'd like to explore.
  • Choose your package Pick a session length for live readings, or the number of questions for a recorded reading. Prices are in AUD.
  • Complete your booking For a live reading, choose an available appointment in Australian Eastern Time. For a recorded reading, submit your questions.
  • Receive your reading Live sessions take place privately on Zoom or Google Meet. Recorded readings are emailed within 3 business days. A written summary is included; recorded readings also include a bonus insight where appropriate.
