The process

How an online reading works

Live on Zoom or Google Meet, or recorded and sent to you. Four steps. Prices in AUD.

  • Choose a format A live conversation, or a recorded reading you can replay.
  • Choose a package 30, 60 or 90 minutes. Or 5, 7 or 11 questions.
  • Book and pay A booking number is emailed as confirmation, then you pay with Stripe. The booking is not held unless payment is received. Live times are in Australian Eastern Time (Sydney).
  • Receive your reading Live on Zoom or Google Meet, or a video within 3 business days of your questions being submitted. Written summary (PDF) included.

Clarity is the first step. You take the next.
